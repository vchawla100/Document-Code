Solution:

As already discussed, IBPS displays the FIRCO status on the form based on the entry available in the database table, which is updated by the FIRCO team. IBPS does not have any control over this status update.
As also shared by your team in the email trail, the status value for these work items is "0000". Accordingly, the status is being displayed as "False Positive" on the form. Hence, the behavior observed is as per the data currently available in the table.
It is required to investigate how the status value "0000" was updated in the table from the FIRCO side, as IBPS only reflects the status available in the database and does not perform any modification to this value.
